# Offline and Sync

Storage rooms often have bad signal, so the app is built to keep working without it.

- **Every phone keeps a full copy** of the inventory it's allowed to see, so searching and browsing work with no internet at all.
- **Changes save on the phone first**: new boxes, items, moves, photos. A dark bar at the top says *No signal — still saving on this phone (3 waiting)*. When the phone is back online, everything sends itself in order.
- **Photos** are shrunk on the phone (about 1600 px, plus a small thumbnail) and uploaded in the background. Photos you've already seen stay available offline.
- **Staying up to date:** the app checks for other people's changes every 20 seconds while it's open, and right away when you come back to it. Only the changes since the last check are downloaded.
- **Two people editing at once:** changes are saved field by field, so Mom renaming a box while Zayne moves it doesn't lose either change.
- **Alerts:** a chime and buzz while the app is open. Turn on **Me → Pop-up alerts on this phone** to also get a system notice when the app is in the background.

If something you did needs someone's OK, the phone doesn't pretend it happened. You see *Sent to Victoria to OK*, and it happens when she says yes.
