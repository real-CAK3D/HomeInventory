# Family Guide

## Signing in

1. Open the app and tap **your face**.
2. Type your 4-number PIN on the big keypad.
3. **First time?** Type `0000`, then pick your own PIN and type it twice.

You stay signed in on that phone. To hand the phone to someone else, go to **Me → Switch person**.

## The five buttons at the bottom

| Button | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Find** | Search everything: type, or tap the microphone and say it |
| **Shelves** | The shelf wall: every section, shelf and box |
| **+ Pack** | Pack a box, or add a single thing |
| **Alerts** | People asking for your OK, and news about your things |
| **Me** | Your picture, text size, and how you want to be told about changes |

## Finding something

Type a few letters: *blend*, *xmas lights*, *mugs*. Small typos are fine. You can also type:

- a box number, like `12` or `box 12`
- a section, like `section 3`, or `3-2` for section 3, shelf 2

Each result shows the path, for example **Section 3 › Middle shelf › Box 1 (Kitchen)**. Tap **Show me** to see that box light up on the shelf wall with a bouncing pin.

## Packing boxes (Pack mode)

Tap the big **+** in the middle.

1. **Where?** Tap the section number, then the shelf.
2. **Snap the shelf.** One photo of that shelf. If the whole section has no photo yet, the app offers that too.
3. **The box.** The box number is filled in for you, so write it big on the box. Add a name like *Kitchen* if you like, pick a color and say whose box it is. Snap the outside.
4. **What's inside.** Open it and snap the contents (that alone helps a lot). Then type the main things, one per line or with commas: `blender, 4 mugs, good knives`.
5. **Done!** Tap **Next box on this shelf** and keep going.

Tip: you don't have to list every fork. A photo of the inside plus the main things is plenty.

## When a shelf changes

Shelf photos are shared. Once anyone snaps a shelf, everyone sees that photo, and Pack mode lets the next person skip it (**Looks the same — next**).

If you reorganize a shelf, or swap a box for a bin, take a new photo with **Update photo** on the section page, or **Shelf changed? Update photo** in Pack mode. The old photo is kept in the shelf's history (tap the shelf photo to swipe through them). The app then asks **Did anything on this shelf move around?**

- **No:** the new photo just replaces the old one.
- **Yes:** you get the **shelf check**. Drag across the before-and-now photo to compare. Then mark each box **Still here**, **Moved** (pick where it went), or **Gone**. Use the pencil to turn a box into a bin, tote or crate. Add anything new with **Move a box here** or **Pack a new one**.

Everyone with things on that shelf gets a notice that it was rearranged. Owners of boxes that moved hear exactly where they went. Locked boxes still need their owner's OK before they move.

## Taking something out

Open the thing and tap **I'm taking this out**. Add a note if you like (*"in the kitchen"*). Everyone can see it's out and who has it. When it's back, tap **I put it back**.

## Moving a box

Open the box → **Move box** → pick the new section and shelf. To move a single thing into another box, open the thing → **Move**.

## Mistakes

Right after any change, a bar at the bottom shows **Undo**. Removed boxes and things go to **Me → Trash**, where you can bring them back.

## Bigger text

**Me → How it looks → Text size → Bigger / Biggest.** You can also pick **Light** or **Dark**, and **Calm** animations if the movement is distracting.
