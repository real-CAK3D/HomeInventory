# Locks and Approvals

Every box and thing has an **owner**: a person, or **Everyone** (shared family stuff).

## Your choices

**Lock it.** Open your box or thing and turn on **Lock**. After that, if anyone else tries to move, edit, take out or remove it (or add something to a locked box), the app doesn't make the change. It sends you a request instead.

**Keep it private.** For a box, others see only that *a private box* sits on that shelf, with none of its contents. A private thing is hidden from everyone else entirely.

**For things you haven't locked**, go to **Me → When others touch your things**:

| Setting | Moves and edits by others | Removing by others |
|---|---|---|
| **Tell me** *(default)* | Happens; you get a notice | — |
| **Ask me first** | Needs your OK | Needs your OK *(default)* |
| **Don't bother me** | Happens quietly | — |
| **Just tell me** (removing) | — | Happens; you get a notice |

Putting something **back** is always allowed. Nobody needs permission to return your stuff.

## Answering a request

The bell at the bottom shows a number. Open **Alerts → Asking you**, read what they want ("Zayne is asking: remove Gold plates — *need for party*"), and tap **Yes, OK** or **No**. When you say no, you can add a reason. They get your answer straight away.

Requests you've sent are under **Alerts → You asked**, where you can cancel one that's still waiting.

## Who can do what

- **Everyone** can add boxes and things, and edit anything not protected by the rules above.
- **Admins** (Andrew) can also add or remove people, reset PINs, build or remove shelf sections, and save a full backup. Admins *don't* get to skip someone else's locks; they ask like everyone else.
- The **server** checks every rule, not just the phone, so locks can't be got around.
